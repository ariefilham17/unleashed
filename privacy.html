Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information unleashed ("the Service") handles when you connect your TikTok account and upload content.

1. Information we handle

From TikTok login: your TikTok open ID, display name, and profile picture (the user.info.basic permission), and an access token that lets the Service upload to your account on your request.

Content you provide: the video file and caption you choose to upload.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to show which TikTok account is connected and to upload the video and caption you selected to your TikTok account as a draft. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Your video and caption are sent to TikTok through its official API, which is the purpose of the Service. We do not share your information with other third parties, except where required by law.

4. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as your account stays connected. Videos are used only to complete your upload and are not kept longer than needed for that purpose.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect unleashed at any time by removing it from the connected apps in your TikTok settings. You can also email us to request deletion of any data we hold about you, and we will act on the request within a reasonable time.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age TikTok requires in your country.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
