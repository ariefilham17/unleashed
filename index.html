Your videos, on TikTok, on your schedule.

unleashed is a web tool for creators. Pick a video, write a caption, and send it to your own TikTok account as a draft, ready for you to review and publish.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok accountLog in with TikTok and approve the permissions unleashed asks for.
  2. Choose a video and write a captionSelect a video file you own and add your caption in the dashboard.
  3. Upload it as a draftunleashed sends the video to your TikTok inbox. You open TikTok, check it, and publish it yourself.

What unleashed can and cannot do

unleashed only uploads content that you choose, to the account that you connect. It never posts without your action, never reads other people's data, and never sells your information. See the Privacy Policy for details.
